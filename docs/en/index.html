LumaFlow

Your light table, reinvented.

Editing becomes a playground again.

Set your photo on the table. Flip through the film stocks with a swipe of your finger — Film, Light, Vignetting — and watch every look come alive before your eyes. Keep what you like. Forget what you don't: the original stays untouched, right underneath, ready to start over as many times as you want.

The spirit of the light table

Zero risk, total freedom Your original photo is untouchable. Try, undo, start over, as many times as you like — it never changes.
New film stocks keep arriving, the table keeps growing The style drawer is never closed: it grows richer over time, without ever disturbing the habits you've already built.
Everything is visible before you decide Every step — Film, Light, Vignetting… — shows you its effect as thumbnails, at a glance. A click applies the style; the magnifier opens a detailed view to fine-tune every slider.
Your style, duplicated in one click One recipe. A thousand photos. Save your settings once, reuse them everywhere.
And for a thousand photos, a single gesture Batch processing applies a preset to a whole list of images while you do something else: live progress, a detailed log, and a single failing file never stops the others.

100% local. 0% cloud.

⚡ Your photos never leave your computer. No upload, no account, no waiting — everything runs locally, from the moment it launches.
