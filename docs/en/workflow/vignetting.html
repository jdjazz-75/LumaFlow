4.7 Vignetting

Engine: a shape (ellipse or band), positioned/sized/rotated in a frame centered on itself, defines a spared "inner" zone; outside it, the image is progressively darkened (or brightened), the transition softened by blur and shaped by a progressivity curve.

Thumbnails

ThumbnailEffect
NeutralNo transformation.
Round centralCentered ellipse covering about 85% of the frame (equal radii), darkening up to 35% toward the corners/edges, soft transition (12% feather), moderate progressivity curve. The classic circular vignette.
LinearA clear horizontal band covering the central 60% of the height, darkening up to 30% on the top/bottom bands (20% each), the same transition softness. A "letterbox" effect darkened at top and bottom.

Manual settings

10 settings in total; 8 are shared by both shapes (Strength, Progressivity, Centre X/Y, Rotation, Feather), 2 are specific to the ellipse (Radius X/Y) and 2 to the band (Top/bottom offset).

  • Strength (intensity, 0..1) — maximum strength of the darkening outside the shape.

  • Progressivity (progressivity, 0..1) — shapes the transition curve: lower, the cutoff is sharper/more abrupt; higher, the gradient is more gradual.

  • Centre X (center_x, -0.5..1.5) — horizontal position of the shape's center (0.5 = center of the image; the range deliberately extends past [0,1] to allow decentering the shape up to half off-frame).

  • Centre Y (center_y, -0.5..1.5) — vertical position of the center, same convention.

  • Radius X (radius_x, 0.05..2.0) — half-width of the ellipse (Round central only).

  • Radius Y (radius_y, 0.05..2.0) — half-height of the ellipse (Round central only).

  • Rotation (rotation_angle, -180..180°) — rotates the shape around its center (no visible effect on a perfect circle — illustrated here on an elongated ellipse to make the rotation perceptible).

  • Feather width (feather_width, 0..40% of the image's smaller dimension) — width of the transition blur: 0 = sharp edge, higher values = a very soft, spread-out transition.

  • Top offset (top_offset, 0.05..1.5, Linear only) — distance from the center to the top edge of the clear band: smaller means the darkened band at the top is wider.

  • Bottom offset (bottom_offset, 0.05..1.5, Linear only) — the same setting for the bottom edge.
